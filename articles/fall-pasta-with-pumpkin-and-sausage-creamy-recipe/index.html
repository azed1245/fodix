Fall Pasta With Pumpkin And Sausage Creamy Recipe

October 20, 2026

When the leaves start to change and the air gets crisp, there is nothing more comforting than a warm bowl of pasta. This Fall Pasta with Pumpkin and Sausage brings together the earthy sweetness of pumpkin and the savory kick of Italian sausage. It is a creamy, dreamy meal that feels like a hug in a bowl, making it the ultimate comfort food for a chilly evening.
